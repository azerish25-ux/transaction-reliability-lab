# E05 — Accessible operational UX

Status: PREPARED, NOT EXECUTED. Risks: R17, R03, R10.

## Mission
Explore whether essential money and investigation workflows remain understandable and operable without a pointer or wide display.

## Setup and entry criteria
Customer/admin at approximately 1440×900, 768×1024 and 390×844; keyboard only; reduced motion; long amounts/references; real loading/error/empty states. The real integrated environment and approved lab controls must first be available; do not substitute mock pages.

## Suggested session scope
One bounded 45-minute exploration session with one primary question; stop immediately on suspected unexplained financial corruption and preserve state. This is a future charter, not an execution log.

## Techniques and heuristics
Complete transfer confirmation, uncertain retry, cancellation/refund, schedule edits and admin ledger investigation by keyboard. Inspect focus trap/return, labels, error summaries, live announcements, scrolling and non-color status. Pair axe results with semantic review.

## Observations and evidence
Capture sanitized screenshots, exact viewport, key sequence, focus before/after dialog, applicable axe findings, truncation/readability issues and real backed data. Store actual session records under `docs/evidence/exploratory/E05/` only after execution, with source SHA, environment, tester type, start/end times and artifact hashes.

## Debrief
Could every critical action be completed? Was focus lost after error/expiry? Were synthetic-money and historical-evidence labels clear? Which screen-reader checks still require human execution? Separate observations from hypotheses and automated evidence from human judgments.
