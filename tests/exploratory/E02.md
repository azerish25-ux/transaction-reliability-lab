# E02 — Contention and recovery

Status: PREPARED, NOT EXECUTED. Risks: R02, R04, R05, R07.

## Mission
Explore correlated concurrency and recovery failures without allowing lost/duplicated economic effects.

## Setup and entry criteria
Two API and two worker instances; 10,000 minor units; simultaneous 8,000 spends; controlled post-commit/pre-ACK worker boundary; disposable broker/database. The real integrated environment and approved lab controls must first be available; do not substitute mock pages.

## Suggested session scope
One bounded 45-minute exploration session with one primary question; stop immediately on suspected unexplained financial corruption and preserve state. This is a future charter, not an execution log.

## Techniques and heuristics
Coordinate requests with a barrier, not arbitrary sleep. Race cancel/settle and refund/spending. Kill only the owned lab worker at the measured boundary. Recover the dependency and observe backlog and consistent-snapshot reconciliation.

## Observations and evidence
Capture process IDs owned by the test, request/response ordering, barriers, DB lock/SQLSTATE evidence, message identities, holds, journal/audit/outbox consistency and cleanup. Store actual session records under `docs/evidence/exploratory/E02/` only after execution, with source SHA, environment, tester type, start/end times and artifact hashes.

## Debrief
Was a controlled business rejection returned rather than an unexplained 500? Could recovery finish within the declared budget after dependencies returned? Did teardown leave an active fault? Separate observations from hypotheses and automated evidence from human judgments.
