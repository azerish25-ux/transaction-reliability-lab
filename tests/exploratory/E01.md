# E01 — Uncertain money movement

Status: PREPARED, NOT EXECUTED. Risks: R01, R03, R09.

## Mission
Test whether users can distinguish a pending/uncertain outcome from a durable rejection and safely resolve the original economic intent.

## Setup and entry criteria
Alice with 10,000 CAD minor units; Bob recipient; logged-in short-lived session; capture a response-loss boundary only in the authorized lab. The real integrated environment and approved lab controls must first be available; do not substitute mock pages.

## Suggested session scope
One bounded 45-minute exploration session with one primary question; stop immediately on suspected unexplained financial corruption and preserve state. This is a future charter, not an execution log.

## Techniques and heuristics
Use deliberate double-clicks, keyboard submission, reload, a second tab and expiry immediately before/after submit. Retry the same amount/recipient/key after the first response is lost. Try editing intent while unresolved; changing owner must not expose prior intent.

## Observations and evidence
Capture original/resent normalized body and key, visible uncertainty text, receipt identity, durable idempotency row, journal count and restored focus. Never record JWTs/cookies. Store actual session records under `docs/evidence/exploratory/E01/` only after execution, with source SHA, environment, tester type, start/end times and artifact hashes.

## Debrief
Did any message falsely declare failure/success? Could a new key accidentally cause a second payment? Was reauthentication independent of intent resolution? Separate observations from hypotheses and automated evidence from human judgments.
