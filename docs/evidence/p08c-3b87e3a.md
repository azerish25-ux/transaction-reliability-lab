## Delivered boundary

P08C supplies recipient-owner partial/full refunds, a narrow administrator payment lookup/full-reversal interface with a mandatory reversal reason, and authoritative adjustment context, paged history and immutable receipts. Exact minor-unit parsing and server-side payment/recipient rules remain authoritative. A customer's ability to read an outgoing payment does not grant refund authority or access to the recipient's unrelated balance.

The interface preserves the original refund/reversal intent and idempotency key when the response is uncertain, including recovery through reload and reauthentication. A refund or reversal creates a compensating adjustment; it does not rewrite the original settlement journal. `SETTLED` remains separate from `NONE`, `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`, `FULLY_REFUNDED` and `REVERSED` adjustment states.
