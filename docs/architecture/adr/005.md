# ADR-005 — Intended wall-clock schedules

Status: Java policy unit-verified; no durable scheduler implementation.

## Decision
Persist intended local date/time and IANA zone separately from next UTC instant. Resolve gaps forward by gap duration; choose earlier offset in overlap. Advance local calendar dates rather than fixed UTC durations.

## Consequences and alternatives
A fixed 24-hour recurrence drifts across daylight-saving transitions. Stable schedule/version/local identity supports future deduplication. Policy correctness does not prove occurrence locking, pause/edit races, 24-hour catchup or restart execution.
